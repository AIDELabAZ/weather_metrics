			\caption{Training and Held-Out Gains From Instrument Search}
			\label{app:gain}
			{\centering
				\includegraphics[width=.97\linewidth,height=5.35in,keepaspectratio]{Figures/gain.pdf}\par}
			\smallskip
			\footnotesize \textit{Note}: Quantity is maize yield; Value is farm
			production value. Gains are the absolute changes in the first-stage
			partial-correlation coefficient from K=1 (the first randomly ordered
			candidate) to K=84 (all candidates), not percentages or t-statistics.
			Training fit is absolute partial correlation; held-out fit retains its
			sign oriented by the selected instrument's training sign. Filled/open
			circles show training/held-out gains; arrows run from filled to open.
			Blue denotes year FE; orange denotes year and household FE. Gains are
			paired within the same allocation, fold and ordering before medians over
			20 orderings per fold and 100 fold/allocation units. Taller/shorter
			whiskers show the respective training/held-out IQRs, with each marker on
			its own interval's centerline. These are descriptive percentiles, not
			confidence intervals. Repeated folds reuse observations. Connectors bend
			slightly for nearly coincident gains to keep the direction visible.
		\end{minipage}
	\end{figure}
\end{landscape}
